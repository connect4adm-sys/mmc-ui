About My Mentor Circle

Built Around Schools. Driven by Student Futures.

My Mentor Circle (MMC) is a school-first educational events marketplace created to make career fairs, university interactions, hands-on STEM workshops, and student development sessions effortless for schools — while keeping every participation decision firmly in the school's hands.

Explore Events Catalogue Connect with Us
School-First Model: Schools host & approve every event; MMC coordinates the ecosystem seamlessly.
Our Mission

Bring Real-World Academic & Career Discovery Inside Every School Campus.

Students shouldn't wait until after board exams to discover what careers, degrees, and skills exist. We empower school principals and counsellors to curate high-value educational events — at zero platform cost to the school.

Our Vision

India's Most Trusted Ecosystem Connecting Schools, Universities & Mentors.

A transparent, structured bridge where K–12 schools retain complete campus governance, universities engage respectfully and authentically, and millions of students graduate with wider horizons and clearer choices.

How We Operate

Four Principles Behind Every MMC Event.

01

100% Free for Schools

Zero subscriptions, zero hidden charges. Schools create, publish, and host events on MMC completely free.

02

School-Led Governance

Principals and academic coordinators approve every university, mentor, and partner before anyone steps on campus.

03

Verified Ecosystem

We work with accredited universities, structured EdTech partners, and credible trainers aligned with student wellbeing.

04

End-to-End Coordination

From scheduling and partner matching to on-ground event flow, MMC reduces administrative workload for school staff.
