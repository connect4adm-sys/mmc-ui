Common Questions

Quick Answers for Schools & Partners.

Is My Mentor Circle genuinely 100% free for schools?
Yes. Creating a school account, publishing educational events, reviewing partner requests, and hosting approved sessions on campus carries zero platform fees for K–12 schools.
Who decides which universities or mentors can enter our school campus?
Your school retains 100% approval authority at every stage. Universities and education partners submit a participation request, and only those explicitly approved by your school leadership are confirmed.
How far in advance should a school schedule a career fair or workshop?
We recommend reaching out 2 to 4 weeks ahead of your preferred date so we can align verified partners, tailor the session agenda to your grade levels, and coordinate campus logistics smoothly.
How can a university or EdTech organisation apply to participate?
Visit the For Universities & EdTech Partners page to browse open school events or submit your institutional onboarding profile directly.
