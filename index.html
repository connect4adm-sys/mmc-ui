How It Works

A Simple Process. Your School Stays in Control.

A transparent 5-step workflow designed to save school leaders time while keeping campus decisions entirely in your hands.

Step 01

School Creates an Event

The school publishes its event details, schedule and participation requirements.

Step 02

Partners Discover the Event

Relevant institutions and education partners explore suitable opportunities.

Step 03

Participation Requests

Interested organisations submit requests to participate.

Step 04

School Reviews and Approves

School leadership reviews requests and retains final approval authority.

Step 05

MMC Coordinates

MMC supports communication and coordination for confirmed participation and events.

100% School Authority

You have full visibility over participating institutions before approving. No outside participant enters your campus without your explicit approval.
